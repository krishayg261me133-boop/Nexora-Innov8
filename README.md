# 🚀 NEXORA

### The Next Generation of Student Collaboration

**NEXORA** is a Campus Talent, Skills & Project Team Formation Network designed to help students discover teammates, collaborate on projects, explore opportunities, and build meaningful connections across campus.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

### Campus Talent, Skills & Project Team Formation Network

Students often struggle to discover talented peers outside their existing friend circles when forming teams for hackathons, research projects, startups, academic initiatives, and campus activities.

Campus talent is often fragmented across departments and communities, making it difficult to identify individuals with the right skills, interests, experience, and availability.

As a result:

- Talent remains undiscovered
- Team formation becomes inefficient
- Opportunities are missed
- Cross-disciplinary collaboration is limited
- Research and innovation potential are underutilized

NEXORA addresses this challenge by providing a single ecosystem where students can discover talent, build teams, collaborate on projects, and connect with opportunities.
